Don't search. Just say it.

Type it or hold to talk. Dial Down understands where it hurts, how many exercises you want and how long you have, then builds a routine you can start right away.

Private, on device

It runs on Apple's on‑device model, and your voice is transcribed offline. What you say stays on your iPhone.

Real routines only

It picks only from the 372‑exercise library, using the same engine as the rest of the app. It never invents a stretch.

Safety comes first

You don't need to know it's your levator scapulae. Just tap where it hurts.

iii.

Get its stretches

Only the stretches that actually work that muscle, each with a demo and steps.

As a guest

Your sessions, routines, streaks, body‑map marks and preferences stay on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, and there's no account to make.

If you sign in (optional)

We back up your name, an account ID, progress totals, session history and routines so you can restore them on another device. Only you can read them, and Delete Account erases them from our servers.

Never leaves your iPhone

What you ask Ask Dial Down. Your voice, which is transcribed on device and never recorded. Your Apple Health and Calendar data. Body‑map marks.

What “no trackers” means

No analytics or advertising SDKs, no ad identifier, no cross‑app tracking. We never sell your data. Details in the Privacy Policy.
